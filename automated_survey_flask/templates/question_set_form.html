Writing open-ended questions

Open-ended questions elicit longer answers — which gives prosody analysis enough net speech to be reliable (≥30 s).

Pattern: start with "Tell me about", "Walk me through", "How", or "What". Avoid starting with "Are", "Did", "Do", "Is", "Will".

❌ Closed (yes/no, one word)✅ Open-ended (elicits 20+ seconds)
Did you sleep well?Tell me about how last night went.
Are you feeling okay?How has your week been so far?
Do you have energy today?What did you get up to yesterday?
Are you eating?Walk me through your meals today.
